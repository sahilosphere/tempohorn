About This Rickshaw

Horn Please is a small browser toy built for anyone who loves the chaos and character of Indian street horns. Every sound is synthesized live using the Web Audio API — no audio files, no downloads, no external API.

Tap a horn, watch the meter climb, and enjoy a bit of filmy street energy from wherever you are.
